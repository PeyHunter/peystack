# Write

Write clean as you draft. A cleanup pass after does not catch these.

**Structure (docs).** Pick one mode per page: tutorial, how-to, reference, or explanation. Lead with what the reader needs. Sentence-case headings that state the task or finding.

**Sentences.** One idea each, under about 25 words. Active voice. Present tense. Second person for instructions. One word per concept, never synonym cycling. Keep articles. A plain verb beats an `-ing` form. Imperative for steps, one action per step. Plain global English, no idioms.

**Cut on sight.**
- Long dashes and en dashes. Use a period or comma.
- Mid-sentence colons. A colon before a list is fine.
- "delve, crucial, pivotal, robust, seamless, leverage, landscape, tapestry, testament, underscore, showcase, foster, enhance, additionally, intricate, vibrant".
- "serves as", "stands as", "boasts". Write "is" or "has".
- "Not just X, but Y". Forced groups of three. False ranges "from X to Y".
- Trailing -ing clauses ("..., ensuring reliability").
- Vague attribution ("experts say"). Name the source or cut it.
- Bold on every term. Bold-label-colon bullets that restate the line.
- Emojis in headings or bullets. Curly quotes.
- Chatbot phrases ("Great question", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if"). Sycophancy.
- Filler ("in order to" becomes "to", "due to the fact that" becomes "because"). Stacked hedges.
- Generic conclusions. End on a fact or a next step.

**Evidence.** Every claim says measured, inferred, or guess. Never invent a link or citation.

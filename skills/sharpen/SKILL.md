name: sharpen
description: Make a task deeper, not bigger, then rewrite the prompt. Use for /sharpen.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Before rewriting the task:

1. What would a top professional here do that I am not asking for?
2. What raw material would they start from instead of what is pre-digested?
3. Which methods and tools would they use?
4. What, other than you or me, would show the result is right?

Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that. Leave choices that are mine open.

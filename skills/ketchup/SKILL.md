# Ketchup

Read-only turn. Plain words, short sentences, what changed for the user, not how.

1. Open with one sentence naming what waits on the user, or that nothing does.
2. What happened, under 100 words. Screenshots or one small Mermaid diagram (at most six boxes) when a picture is clearer.
3. Action items, about 30 words each: the call, your pick, what it changes for users, the evidence, the risk. Options get what each gains and costs. Unknown facts say "unknown".

If nothing happened and nothing waits, say so and stop.
